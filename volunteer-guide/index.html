Adding photos and videos 如何添加照片和视频

You do not need to know any code. Everything happens in the Weebly editor, which works like a word processor — you drag things onto the page and type.

完全不需要任何编程知识。所有操作都在 Weebly 编辑器里完成，它的用法就像 文字处理软件：把元素拖到页面上，然后直接输入。

Nothing you do is live until you click Publish.
You can click around, try things, and change your mind. The website only changes at the moment you press the Publish button. 在你点击「Publish 发布」之前，网站不会有任何改变。可以放心尝试。

Before you start 开始之前

  • You need an invitation. An EmP admin sends it from the site's Settings → Editors. You will get an email with a link — open that link and set a password. 你需要一封邀请邮件。管理员从网站的 Settings → Editors 发送， 你会收到带链接的邮件，打开后设置密码即可。
  • Photos straight from a phone are fine. No need to resize or edit anything first. Avoid uploading RAW camera files. 手机拍的照片直接用就可以，不需要先修改尺寸。请不要上传相机的 RAW 原始文件。
  • Pick your best photos, not all of them. Eight to fifteen good photos tell the story better than eighty. 挑选最好的照片，不必全部上传。八到十五张精选照片，比八十张更能讲好故事。

Adding photos 添加照片

  1. Open the page you want to update and click Edit. 打开要更新的页面，点击 Edit（编辑）。
  2. Find the block of photos on the page and click it once. This is a Gallery — one element that holds all the photos for that activity. 找到页面上的照片区域，单击一次。这是一个「Gallery 图库」元素， 该活动的所有照片都放在里面。
  3. Click Upload Images in the toolbar that appears. 在弹出的工具栏中点击 Upload Images（上传图片）。
  4. Choose the photos from your computer. You can select many at once by holding Shift while clicking. 从电脑中选择照片。按住 Shift 键可一次选中多张。
  5. Wait for the upload to finish. The photos appear in the gallery and the layout arranges itself — you do not have to line anything up. 等待上传完成。照片会自动排列，无需手动调整版式。
  6. Click Publish at the top right. 点击右上角的 Publish（发布）。

Writing captions 添加图片说明

  1. Click the gallery, then click the single photo you want to caption. 点击图库，再点击想要添加说明的那张照片。
  2. Type into the Caption field on the right. 在右侧的 Caption（说明）栏中输入文字。
  3. Keep it short: what is happening, and when. Both languages if you can — for example “Tug-of-war, June 2026 拔河比赛，2026年6月”. 尽量简短：写清楚是什么活动、什么时间。可以的话中英文都写， 例如「Tug-of-war, June 2026 拔河比赛，2026年6月」。

Reordering and removing 调整顺序与删除

  • To reorder: drag a photo to a new position inside the gallery. Put the strongest photo first — it is the one most people will see. 调整顺序：在图库内拖动照片即可。把最好的一张放在最前面， 因为多数人只会看到它。
  • To remove: click the photo, then the delete icon. This only removes it from the web page; the original on your own computer is untouched. 删除：点击照片后点删除图标。这只会从网页上移除， 你电脑里的原图不受影响。

Adding a video 添加视频

  1. Put the video on YouTube first. If you do not want it to show up in search results, set it to Unlisted — anyone with the link can still watch it. 先把视频上传到 YouTube。如果不希望被搜索到， 可设为 Unlisted（不公开列出）——有链接的人仍然可以观看。
  2. Copy the video's link from your browser's address bar. 从浏览器地址栏复制视频链接。
  3. In the Weebly editor, look in the left sidebar under Multimedia and drag the YouTube element onto the page, below the photos. 在 Weebly 编辑器左侧栏的 Multimedia（多媒体）中， 把 YouTube 元素拖到页面上、照片的下方。
  4. Paste the link into the Video URL box. 把链接粘贴到 Video URL 栏中。
  5. Click Publish. 点击 Publish（发布）。

Please do not change 请勿改动

Three things on these pages are built in code.
The coloured card blocks, the page names, and the menu. If one of those looks wrong, tell an admin rather than editing it — a small change there can break the links between pages. 页面上有三样东西是用代码搭建的：彩色卡片区块、页面名称、导航菜单。 如果发现问题，请告诉管理员，不要自行修改——改动可能会破坏页面之间的链接。

If something goes wrong 出错了怎么办

  • Before you published: nothing happened. Close the editor without publishing and the live site stays exactly as it was. 如果还没发布：什么都没有发生。直接关闭编辑器，网站保持原样。
  • After you published: tell an admin straight away, and say what you were doing. Do not try to fix it by deleting things — that usually makes it harder to put back. 如果已经发布：请立即告诉管理员，并说明你当时的操作。 不要试图通过删除来修复，那通常会更难恢复。
