Diverse Parenting · Unified Community 多元化的育儿 · 联合化的社区

Emerald Parents Association (EmP) is a grassroots parents' group, founded in 2007. Our mission is to build a strong, healthy family community for Chinese families in the Greater Seattle area — to strengthen parenting and family values, and to organize community activities. We share community information and exchange parenting experiences. We organize education and community activities for our families, our busy parents, our children, and even our children's grandparents. On December 21, 2014, EmP was officially registered as a nonprofit organization in Washington State. Today, EmP has more than 200 association member families and maintains a mailing list of over 600 general members — and she continues to grow. Emerald Parents Association (EmP) 是一个民间父母团体，成立于2007年，宗旨是为基于大西雅图的华人，建设健康有力的家庭社区，提升育儿和家庭观念，组织社区活动。分享社区信息，交流育儿经验。我们组织各类教育，社区活动，为我们的家庭，忙碌的父母，我们的孩子，甚而孩子们的祖父母。在2014年12月21日，EmP正式注册为华州非营利性组织。今天，EmP已经有两百多家协会会员家庭，维护一个有着超过六百的普通会员的邮件列表。她仍然在不断发展壮大之中。
