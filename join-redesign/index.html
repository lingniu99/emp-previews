Join Us

也许您刚刚踏上西雅图这片土地不久，迫切希望了解华人社区的信息；也许您的孩子在您的怀抱里茁壮成长，您也有很多为人父母的心得体会。那么，来吧，加入到EmP的大家庭里来，我们一起欢笑，我们一起成长。

Perhaps you’ve recently set foot in Seattle, eager to learn about the Chinese community. Perhaps your child is thriving in your embrace, and you have valuable parenting experiences. Well then, come join the EmP family. Let’s laugh together, let’s grow together.

Association Member协会会员 — the core of EmP 组织的核心
For general members ready to take a bigger part in the family. 适合希望更深入参与的普通会员。
