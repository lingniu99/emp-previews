# Contact Us — suggested layout

All native Weebly elements, no embed code: this is the content most likely to
change, and it should never need regenerating.

Order follows what a visitor came to do: act first, then reach you another
way, then follow, then the specialised stuff.

```
Title            Contact Us 联系我们

Text             intro — what the form is for, who reads it, when to expect a reply

[ Two columns ]
  left           Form  (the existing one, unchanged)
  right          Text — Email  /  Mail

Divider

Text             Follow us 关注我们  — the five channels
Image            the WeChat QR already on the page
Text             Newsletter — one line, linking to Become a Member

Divider

Text             EmP HackFest — its own three channels
```

Weebly stacks two-column rows on a phone, so the form lands above the contact
details there, which is the right order on mobile too.

Keep HackFest last and under its own heading. It is run by the student team
and answers hackathon questions; mixed in above, a parent asking about
membership ends up in a Discord server full of teenagers.

---

## Element 2 — intro

**Decide the reply time before pasting.** Promise the slowest one you are
confident of; a contact page that over-promises is worse than one that
under-promises.

> Questions, comments or suggestions — send us a message with the form below,
> or email us directly. Every message is read by a member of the EmP board,
> and we usually reply within **[a few days / one week / two weeks]**.
>
> 如有任何问题、意见或建议，欢迎填写下方表单，或直接发送邮件给我们。
> 所有留言都会由协会理事会成员查看，我们通常会在**[几天 / 一周 / 两周]**内回复。

*(Confirm who actually monitors the form — the line above assumes the board.)*
