Youth Dance & Instrument Club 舞蹈与器乐社

Still to add: New page. The dance half below is drawn from the current weekly-activities announcement.
EmP 舞蹈社 runs a Saturday morning workshop series taught by EmP teenagers — ballet, K-pop, jazz and modern, and Chinese dance, for ages four and up. Sessions are small, no experience is needed, and the aim is to help a child find the style that suits them before committing to outside classes.
EmP舞蹈社每周六上午开设体验系列课程，由协会青少年小老师授课，涵盖芭蕾、K-pop、爵士与现代舞、中国舞，适合4岁以上。小班教学、零基础即可参加，目的是帮助孩子在报名校外课程前找到适合自己的舞种。
