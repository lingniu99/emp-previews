Diverse Parenting · Unified Community 多元化的育儿 · 联合化的社区

Founded in 2007, Emerald Parents Association unites Chinese families in the Greater Seattle area — building a healthy, supportive community, strengthening parenting and family values, and organizing education and community activities for families, parents, and children. 成立于2007年，翡翠父母协会（EmP）聚集大西雅图地区华人家庭，共同建设积极、健康、有力的社区，提升育儿和家庭理念，组织多样化的教育和社区活动。

  • Seattle-area Chinese family community since 2007
  • Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit
  • 200+ member families · 1,000+ on our list
